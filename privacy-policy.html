Privacy Policy

Kotiki respects your privacy.

Information We Collect

Kotiki may store gameplay progress locally on your device.

If enabled by the player, the game may also use optional cloud save services such as:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firestore cloud save synchronization
  • Sign in with Apple
  • Google Sign-In

In-App Purchases

Kotiki offers optional in-app purchases through Apple’s App Store.

Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. Kotiki does not store payment information.

Analytics & Diagnostics

Basic diagnostics or crash information may be collected to improve app stability and gameplay experience.

Data Sharing

Kotiki does not sell personal data to third parties.

Your Choices

You may stop using cloud save features at any time by not signing in or by removing the app from your device.
